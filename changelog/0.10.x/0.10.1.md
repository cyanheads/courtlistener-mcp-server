# 0.10.1 — 2026-11-20

## Added

- **Docket entries** accept inclusive filing dates and newest-first order. Keep the filters and order when continuing a selected document. ([#99](https://github.com/cyanheads/courtlistener-mcp-server/issues/99))
- **Docket search and listings** expose assigned and referred judge person IDs. Search samples include the filing’s short description. ([#101](https://github.com/cyanheads/courtlistener-mcp-server/issues/101))
- **Court lookup** supports `records: false` for snapshot-only IDs and reports live-page status when an upstream rate limit or outage prevents fetching records. ([#97](https://github.com/cyanheads/courtlistener-mcp-server/issues/97))

## Changed

- **Citation lookup** clamps `max_court_lookups` to 20 and reports the reduced budget. ([#98](https://github.com/cyanheads/courtlistener-mcp-server/issues/98))

## Fixed

- **Readable opinion and filing text** preserves structural breaks and normalizes source line wrapping within paragraphs. ([#95](https://github.com/cyanheads/courtlistener-mcp-server/issues/95))
- **Judge names** include middle names and suffixes when the source omits the derived full name. ([#96](https://github.com/cyanheads/courtlistener-mcp-server/issues/96))
- **Docket entry pagination** stops at page 100, shows remaining entries when the upstream count is known, and identifies out-of-range entry pages. ([#99](https://github.com/cyanheads/courtlistener-mcp-server/issues/99))
- **Attorney rosters** can resume with `attorney_cursor`. Keep the same parties page and merge resolved details by attorney ID. ([#100](https://github.com/cyanheads/courtlistener-mcp-server/issues/100))
- **CourtListener guide** 1.1 → 1.2 documents docket filters, roster continuation, and judge ID handoffs.

## Dependencies

- `@biomejs/biome` `2.6.7` → `2.6.8`.
- `ignore` `^7.0.14` → `^7.0.16`.
- `vitest` `^5.1.0` → `^5.1.1` (thread pool for Bun and Node test runs).
